Client Certificate Vault & CSRs

Manage client keypairs, incoming certificate signing requests, and issued X.509 certificates

$ openssl x509 -req -in clients/[id]/[id].csr -CA ca/root_ca.crt -CAkey ca/root_ca.key -CAcreateserial -out clients/[id]/[id].crt -days 365 -sha256

Untrusted CA Detection

Verifies how OpenSSL rejects rogue attacker certificates outside the trusted root chain

$ openssl verify -CAfile ca/root_ca.crt clients/[client]/fake_[client].crt
Verification Failure: unable to get local issuer certificate (Untrusted CA caught!)

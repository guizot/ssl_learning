Root Certificate Authority (Root CA)

The Root CA is the anchor of trust in Web2 PKI. It signs certificates for clients to prove identity.

$ openssl req -new -x509 -key ca/root_ca.key -out ca/root_ca.crt -days 365 -subj "/CN=MyRootCA/O=GlobalTrustAuthority/C=ID"

Untrusted / Fake CA Attack Detection

Verify how OpenSSL verifies trust boundaries when a rogue attacker attempts to sign unauthorized certificates.

$ openssl verify -CAfile ca/root_ca.crt clients/[client]/fake_[client].crt
✕ Verification Failure: unable to get local issuer certificate (Untrusted CA caught!)

Security Principle: In Web2 PKI, clients reject any certificate whose issuer cannot be chained back to a trusted Root CA in the system's trust store.

Send Encrypted Document to Another Client

Lock a secret document using the recipient's public certificate. It will be delivered directly to the recipient's Inbox.

$ openssl cms -encrypt -in document.txt -out msg.enc recipient.crt
